## Getting there

I had planned an itinerary and abandoned it within the hour, which was the right call. By the time we reached the far end, the light had gone soft and the crowds had thinned. I went to Rayar's Mess specifically for the idli. A short walk turned into a long one because the street kept being interesting. Rayar's Mess was busier than I expected, though the queue moved faster than it looked.
## The place itself

Getting around Chennai on foot was slower and considerably better than the alternative. Prices had changed since the last time, which surprised nobody but me. The Chennai traffic made the short route slower than the long one. Rayar's Mess was the stop I had planned, and the dosa was the reason. The plan lasted about an hour before the walk from the station sent us down a different street entirely.
## What I ate

There is a particular tiredness that comes from a good day of walking, and this was that kind of day. Arriving before seven meant Rayar's Mess was still calm and the staff had time to talk. I would come back to Chennai on a weekday, earlier, and with fewer plans. I asked at the counter rather than trusting the map, and got better directions.
## Notes for next time

Breakfast took longer than planned and was the best decision of the day. Was it worth the detour? Easily — the ride back alone justified the extra half hour.
