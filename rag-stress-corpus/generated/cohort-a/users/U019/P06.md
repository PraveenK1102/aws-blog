## The design

Making the failure reproducible locally took longer than repairing it. The config that caused it had been in place for months without incident. I assumed the database was the bottleneck for two days longer than the evidence supported.

On the instrumented run, AWS showed 12% change. One badly scoped timeout propagated into three services before anyone noticed.
