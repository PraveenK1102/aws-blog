## Getting there

At Anna Tiffin Centre I ordered the idli and it lived up to the recommendation. Arriving before seven meant Anna Tiffin Centre was still calm and the staff had time to talk. Prices and opening hours shift often enough around Hyderabad that I would check again before relying on them.

There was a queue by eight, so the early start justified itself. By the time we reached the far end, the light had gone soft and the crowds had thinned.
## The place itself

Local advice beat everything I had bookmarked, particularly about when to arrive at Anna Tiffin Centre. People kept recommending Anna Tiffin Centre, but I did not make it there this time — no visit. Because the heat arrived earlier than forecast, we rearranged the afternoon around shade rather than sights.

Walking back the long way through Hyderabad showed me a stretch of road I had completely missed.

## What I ate

I went to Anna Tiffin Centre specifically for the dosa. I nearly walked past the entrance twice; there is no signage worth mentioning. The Hyderabad traffic made the short route slower than the long one.

## Notes for next time

I had three places on the list and reached one of them properly. Was it worth the detour? Easily — the walk from the station alone justified the extra half hour. Breakfast took longer than planned and was the best decision of the day. I asked at the counter rather than trusting the map, and got better directions.
