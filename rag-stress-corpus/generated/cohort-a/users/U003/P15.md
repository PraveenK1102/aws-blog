## How I prepared

The part of my stack I lean on most is vector search. Half my notes from July are about communication rather than anything technical. I stopped applying broadly in July; the reply rate went up almost immediately.

## The round itself

My preparation now splits evenly between Spring fundamentals and talking through the project end to end. Something I keep getting wrong: I describe what I built before explaining why it needed building. I have started treating each rejection as a list of gaps instead of a verdict on the Full Stack track. Most of this work sits on FastAPI.

## What I took from it

Scheduling practice at the same hour each morning removed most of the friction of starting. Keeping a list of the questions that made me pause has been more useful than any course. Looking back at July, the useful hours were the ones I spent explaining vector search out loud.
